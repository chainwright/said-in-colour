HOW WE KNOW

1

The try-on

YouCam's AI Clothes (cloth-v4, upper body) puts the shirt on the photo. Everything after that is arithmetic you can check.

2

Your view

Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes (2009) model, applied in linear RGB to every pixel of the render. It simulates a typical colour-blind eye, not yours exactly.

3

The words

We name each shirt from its own colour, as everyone sees it and as you do; the try-on adds whether your light makes it read darker (nearest of 36 menswear colour names by CIEDE2000). Different names and a gap of 8 or more: TRAP. 4 to 8: CAREFUL.

Cosmetic shopping aid, not a medical test. The example man and shirts are AI-generated; the try-ons are real YouCam renders.
